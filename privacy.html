Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Journey Reader ("the app", "we", "us") is developed and operated by GodwinB, based in the Philippines. This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices you have. Questions can be sent to knowthyread@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • There are no accounts, logins, cloud sync, or backups. Your data stays on your device.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools at this time.
  • If you choose the online AI voice, the text being read is sent to a server to create the audio. The offline voices never leave your device.
  • The app offers optional tips and a monthly subscription. Payments are handled by Google Play, and we never receive your card details.
  • We do not sell your data.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is stored locally and is not sent to us:

  • Your library: books, PDFs, and saved web articles you import, plus reading progress, highlights, and settings.
  • Browser data: history, bookmarks, shortcuts, and cookies from the built-in browser.
  • Preferences: theme, voice, soundscape, and timer choices.

You can delete books, clear browser history and cookies, and remove bookmarks in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all of this data from your device.

3. The online AI voice

Journey Reader offers offline voices and an online voice. When you use the online voice, the passage of text being read aloud is sent over the internet to a third-party text-to-speech service, which returns the audio. We do not attach your name, email, or account to these requests because the app has none. The service provider may process the text under its own privacy policy. Do not use the online voice for text you consider confidential; use an offline voice instead.

4. Information we do not collect

At this time we do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers for tracking, or payment details such as card numbers. We do not run analytics, ads, or crash-reporting services. If we add any of these later, we will update this policy first and, where required, ask for your consent.

When you make a purchase, Google Play gives the app purchase information, such as the product purchased, the order ID, and the purchase status, so the app can confirm the purchase. This information is not linked to your name or email by us.

5. Built-in browser and third-party sites

The built-in browser loads sites directly, such as search engines, novel sites, and free book libraries. Those sites may set cookies and collect data under their own policies, which we do not control. The ad blocker reduces some tracking and ads but cannot guarantee protection on every site.

6. Purchases

The app is free to download and use. It offers optional in-app purchases: one-time tips and a monthly subscription to support development. All payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card number, billing address, or other payment details. Google handles that information under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms.

Your purchases are tied to your Google account on Google Play. You can view and manage them, including canceling the subscription, in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions.

7. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We share data only with the online voice provider as described above, with Google Play as needed to process purchases, or when required by law.

8. Data security and retention

Because your data is stored on your device, its security depends on your device's protections, such as a screen lock. We do not keep a copy of your library or history, so we cannot recover it if you lose your device or delete the app.

9. Children

Journey Reader is intended for people aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have rights to be informed, to access, to object, to correct, and to request erasure of your personal data, subject to the law. Since we hold no personal data about you, most of these can be exercised directly in the app. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California, you may have similar rights under local law. You may also contact the Philippine National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph) with concerns.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the app or on this website.
